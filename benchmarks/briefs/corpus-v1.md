# University insolvency — context

## What I'm investigating

I want to understand how Laurentian University came to seek creditor protection in February 2021,
what its own published financial reports said about its finances in the years before, and what the
court process has meant for its staff, its pensions and its programs.

## Key questions I'm trying to answer

- What did the university's financial reports say about its operating results and its debt before the filing?
- What were the board and senior leadership told about the university's finances, and when?
- Who are the monitor, the lenders and the other parties in the court process, and what has the court approved?
- What happens to employee pensions and benefits during the court process?

## Documents I'm expecting or looking for

- Court orders and the monitor's reports in the creditor-protection proceeding.
- The university's annual financial reports.
