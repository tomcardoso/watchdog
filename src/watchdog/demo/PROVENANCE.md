# Demo corpus provenance

Both documents are works of the United States federal government (17 U.S.C. § 105) and
in the public domain. They are bundled as a demonstration corpus for `watchdog demo`.

| File | Source | Fetched |
|---|---|---|
| sec-v-fastow-complaint-excerpt.pdf | https://www.sec.gov/litigation/complaints/comp17762.htm (excerpt: caption, Summary, Defendant, Entities, RADR, Chewco, Southampton, Prayer for Relief) | 2026-07-11 |
| sec-litigation-release-18543.txt | https://www.sec.gov/litigation/litreleases/lr18543.htm | 2026-07-11 |

The complaint excerpt is re-typeset (Courier, rasterized, image-only PDF) so the demo
exercises Watchdog's OCR path; the wording is verbatim from the SEC filing.
