CANDIDATE CHECKLIST — the following spans were harvested mechanically from the document text, each from the page shown. The checklist is a verification aid, not a list of facts or entities to emit: materiality under the domain skill still decides what becomes a fact, the ENTITIES rules still decide what becomes an entity, and on a dense page most candidates are ordinary table cells that belong in no fact at all. Use it two ways: (1) before finishing a page, scan its candidates for anything material you have not yet captured — a buried one-line disclosure, a figure in prose after a table, a name in a signature block; (2) when a fact uses a number, date, or docket number, copy the exact value from the checklist/page rather than paraphrasing.

The [person], [organization] and [location] candidates come from a small automatic name-finder and are rougher than the rest. They are incomplete: it looks only for those three kinds, so assets and proceedings never appear and a public body appears only if it happened to be tagged an organization; it leaves out names it is unsure of; a name that recurs on many pages is listed only on the first few; and on a crowded page these are the first candidates cut. A name missing from the checklist therefore tells you nothing. Its tags are not this schema's types: choose each entity's type from the ENTITIES definitions (an [organization] may be a `public-body`, a [location] is a `place`). Its spans can also be wrong — cut short, missing part of the name, or running into a title or a neighbouring word — so take an entity's name, and any name in a fact, from the page itself, never from the checklist.

Never emit a fact or create an entity merely because a candidate exists, never restate a table row by row, and never invent a value that is not in the document.
