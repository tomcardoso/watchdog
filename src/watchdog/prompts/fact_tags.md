Each numbered item below is a tag that a document's facts carry to say which entity they are about, but which names none of the entities the document defines. The item shows the tag, up to three facts that carry it, and the document's entities. For each item, say which of the document's entities the tag means, or that none of them is it.

Return `answers`: one `{"item": <number>, "match": "..."}` per item. `match` is the id of the entity the tag means, copied exactly as listed (the part before the dash), or "" when none of them is it.

Match when the tag plainly names one of the listed entities: an abbreviation or acronym of its name or an alias ("cvu" for Calder Valley University), its name in another form or language, a shortened or longer form, or a misspelling. Read the facts: they show who or what the tag is about.

Never match a different thing with a similar name: a parent company and its subsidiary, a person and a company named after them, two people who share a surname, a body and one of its committees, a court and a judge. When the facts do not show which entity is meant, or you are not sure, answer "". A wrong match files the fact under the wrong entity; no match keeps it in the document's own record only.
