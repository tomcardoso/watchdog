MACHINE TRANSCRIPT — this document is an automatic speech-to-text transcript of an audio or video recording, not a written document. Each page is five minutes of the recording, and each `[hh:mm:ss]` marks where the following words begin; cite the page as usual. The transcript has no speaker labels, so never attribute words to a named speaker unless the speech itself identifies them (an introduction, someone addressing them by name); otherwise describe who spoke generically ("a speaker said"). Speech recognition mishears: names, numbers, amounts and technical terms may be wrong or spelled phonetically. Copy them as transcribed, and when a name or figure looks implausible or inconsistent with the rest of the recording, say in the fact that it is as transcribed and should be checked against the recording.
